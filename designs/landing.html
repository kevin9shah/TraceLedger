Dossier Standards & Evidentiary Restraint

TraceLedger enforces strict vocabulary and architectural guardrails to ensure reports remain objective evidence for a human investigator rather than automated accusations:

• Risk Indicator, Not a Verdict

The system flags discrepancies and contradictions; it never claims to detect fraud or criminal behavior.

• Read-Only Architecture

TraceLedger never holds private keys, never signs transactions, never moves funds, and never edits accounting ledgers.

• Unresolved is a First-Class Outcome

When records are ambiguous or incomplete, the system abstains as UNRESOLVED and lists the exact missing exhibits that would settle it.

• Quarantined Untrusted Inputs

Business memos, invoice free text, and external data are escaped and treated strictly as data, never as LLM execution instructions.
