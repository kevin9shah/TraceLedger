ACT 6 — MANY EXCEPTIONS, ONE CAUSE.  (RECORDED run, not a live query.)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    18 invoices are flagged across the case load.
    One graph traversal groups them by where their records came from:

      IMP-TOKEN-MAP-01     5 invoices   C04-1,C05-1,C06-1,C07-1,C25-1
      IMP-VENDOR-03        3 invoices   C12-1,C13-1,C14-1
      IMP-REIMPORT-02      3 invoices   C16-DUP,C17-1,C18-1

    11 of 18 exceptions collapse into 3 causes: a bad token-mapping import,
    a vendor-master update that changed destinations, and a month-end job that ran
    twice. That is three things to fix, not eighteen investigations.

  And the honest part, which we put on the slide ourselves:
      SQL returns the IDENTICAL result in 0.1 ms; the graph took 280.0 ms.
      SQL is 2792x faster at this scale. We do not claim the
      graph is faster, nor that it finds anything SQL misses on this question.

THE NUMBERS.  (RECORDED benchmark run, 25 synthetic cases.)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    model: gpt-4o-mini   cases: 25

    metric                                       rules_only       plain_llm     traceledger
    outcome accuracy                            68% (17/25)     64% (16/25)    100% (25/25)
    named the right issue                        41% (7/17)     71% (12/17)    100% (17/17)
    said 'fine' when it wasn't                   32% (8/25)      24% (6/25)       0% (0/25)
    abstained when it should                       0% (0/4)        0% (0/4)      100% (4/4)

  Two things we say out loud before anyone asks:
      1. Our 100% is circular — we wrote the cases and the system. It means the
         benchmark has stopped discriminating, not that the system is perfect.
      2. The plain LLM BEATS the rules baseline at naming the right issue,
         71% to 41%. Claiming LLMs are simply worse at this would be false.

    The figure that matters: 'said fine when it wasn't'. That is the error that
    closes an unpaid invoice.
