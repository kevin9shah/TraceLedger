Forensic Edge Taxonomy & Verification States

STANDARD CODESET V2.4

TraceLedger models stablecoin payouts as an unbroken causal thread. Every state pairs a visible glyph, color token, and explicit label:

✓ Proven / Verified

Verified by cryptographic proof, bytecode examination, and on-chain consensus confirmation.

⚠ Contradicted

Direct conflict detected between internal accounting records and on-chain token event parameters.

☉ Asserted / Candidate

Pending dual-custody approval, secondary multisig signature, or unverified business record link.

◌ Missing

Missing evidence in the audit trail; critical intermediate hops or counterparty attestations unlocated.

FORENSIC ARCHITECTURE

The 5-Stage Evidentiary Protocol

TraceLedger replaces flat reconciliation with a multi-hop proof graph. Every layer can only refine and substantiate the findings above it, never override deterministic facts:

Stage 1
Deterministic Verification

Fetches transaction status, integer transfer amounts, and contract decimals directly from live RPC node bytecode. Zero LLM involvement.

Stage 2
Evidence Graph Linking

Constructs a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) in TigerGraph linking invoices, payment requests, approved address history, and ledger entries.

Stage 3
Multi-Hop Query Traversal

Runs pre-installed GSQL queries to isolate high-risk patterns: destination address reuse, recent changes before payment, and shared root causes.

Stage 4
Constrained Narrative Layer

A hosted language model analyzes hypotheses strictly gated by an independent validator: every claim must cite real node IDs.

Stage 5
Human Review Sign-Off

An operations reviewer commits a determination (Confirm, Reject, Request Evidence, or Unresolved) with rationale into an append-only SQLite log (not tamper-proof).

Dossier Standards & Evidentiary Restraint

TraceLedger enforces strict vocabulary and architectural guardrails to ensure reports remain objective evidence for a human investigator rather than automated accusations:

  • Risk indicator, not a verdict about intent: The system flags discrepancies and contradictions; it never claims to detect fraud or criminal behavior.
  • Read-only architecture: TraceLedger never holds private keys, never signs transactions, never moves funds, and never edits accounting ledgers.
  • Insufficient evidence is a first-class outcome: When records are ambiguous or incomplete, the system abstains as UNRESOLVED and lists the exact missing exhibits that would settle it.
  • Untrusted inputs are quarantined: Business memos, invoice free text, and external data are escaped and treated strictly as data, never as LLM execution instructions.
